# ApnoMart — Business Intelligence Dashboard

## Project Overview

The Insight Board is a Business Intelligence project developed for the ApnoAI Data Analytics Internship. It analyzes ApnoMart's sales data to identify revenue trends, profitability patterns, regional performance, and customer purchasing behavior.

The dashboard was built using Power BI Desktop to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights for management decision-making.

## Tools and Technologies

- Power BI Desktop
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- CSV Dataset
- Data Visualization and Business Intelligence

## Dataset

The ApnoMart sales dataset contains 1,200 distinct orders and includes information about order dates, regions, cities, product categories, customer segments, quantities, sales, discounts, profits, and payment methods.

## Dashboard Features

- KPI cards for Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, and Profit Margin
- Sales by Category bar chart
- Monthly Sales Trend line chart
- Total Profit by Region bar chart
- Profit Margin by Category chart
- Payment Method Mix donut chart
- Interactive Region slicer
- Calculated Profit Margin % measure

## Key Performance Indicators

- **Total Sales:** ₹16.26 million
- **Total Profit:** ₹4.42 million
- **Total Orders:** 1,200
- **Overall Profit Margin:** 27.18%

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Revenue Pattern

Furniture is the leading category by sales, contributing approximately ₹3.52 million, or 21.63% of total revenue. July recorded the highest monthly sales in the period analyzed.

**Business implication:** Inventory planning and marketing campaigns should consider category demand and monthly sales patterns.

### 2. Profitability Pattern

Office Supplies achieved the highest profit margin at 34.80%, while Electronics recorded the lowest category profit margin at 18.88%. The Central region generated the highest total profit among the regions.

**Business implication:** Review Electronics pricing, product costs, and discount strategies while exploring opportunities to grow higher-margin categories.

### 3. Customer Behavior

The Corporate customer segment contributed approximately ₹4.31 million in sales, representing 26.51% of total revenue. Cash was the leading payment method by sales value, contributing approximately 21.98%.

**Business implication:** Targeted Corporate customer promotions and continued support for different payment options may help improve customer retention.

## Strategic Recommendation

Prioritize profitable growth by strengthening high-margin category opportunities, investigating the cost and discount drivers behind Electronics profitability, and developing retention initiatives for Corporate customers. Monitor the results using sales, profit, and profit margin KPIs.
